How It Works

From Voice to Vision in Seconds

A seamless creative loop between you and the AI.

1

Speak Your Story

Describe a scene, set the mood, or introduce a character. The AI listens through a real-time audio stream.

2

AI Generates Media

The StoryDirector agent calls Imagen, Veo, and Lyria to create images, clips, and music for each scene.

3

Watch It Unfold

Generated art, video, and narration appear on your cinematic canvas with smooth transitions.

4

Collaborate & Iterate

Change direction, add twists, show props on camera. The story evolves with every interaction.
