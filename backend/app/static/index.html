Flow

From Voice to Vision in Seconds

A single WebSocket connection carries your voice and camera to the AI agent, which orchestrates everything in real-time.

01

You Speak

Your microphone captures audio as raw 16-bit PCM at 16kHz. Camera frames (optional) stream at 2-4 fps as JPEG. Everything flows through one WebSocket.

02

The Agent Thinks

Gemini Live API processes your voice + vision simultaneously. The StoryDirector agent decides which tools to call — image, video, music, or search — based on narrative context.

03

Media Generates

Imagen 4 creates storyboard frames in ~5s. Veo 3.1 generates video clips. Lyria 2 composes mood music. All stored in Cloud Storage and streamed to you.

04

You Experience

The agent narrates with a warm voice while visuals materialize on your Story Canvas. A living storyboard builds scene by scene on your timeline.
